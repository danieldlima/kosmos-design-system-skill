# Kunumi Brand Foundations

Use these extracted rules for any Kunumi visual artifact. Original assets and channel templates remain the authority when their rules are narrower.

## Identity Router

| Identity | Use | Audience and examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core Kunumi | General Kunumi communication; the shared minimum denominator | Internal teams, general stakeholders, company-wide presentations and events |
| Kunumi Unlimited | Work explicitly tied to the LTDA / technology-application identity and Bradesco initiatives | Bradesco-facing presentations, actions, events, dashboards, reports, and stationery |
| Instituto Kunumi | Academic, scientific, research, and Instituto initiatives | Research content, academic events, institutional storytelling |

Do not mix lockups or expressive systems unless the user explicitly requests a co-branded treatment.

## Exact Color System

### Primary: prefer for text, backgrounds, and simple charts

| Token | Hex | Default role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urucum | `#F04E44` | Primary accent, headings, emphasis, calls to action |
| Chumbo | `#1C2127` | Main text, dark background, positive core/Instituto marks |
| Grafite | `#5E5E5E` | Secondary text and supporting neutral |
| Concreto | `#B4ADA4` | Rules, grids, inactive structure, quiet neutral |
| Gelo | `#F0F0F0` | Main light background and negative mark color |

White `#FFFFFF` and Black `#000000` are supporting neutrals.

Near-Urucum values such as `#FF4A3D` and `#FF4B3E` belong to specific exported assets. Preserve them only inside those source files; do not replace `#F04E44` globally.

Instituto artwork contains its own indigo-violet-red-orange-black gradients. Keep those colors inside the supplied artwork instead of rebuilding or generalizing them.

For the deck-extracted dark surfaces, expressive Urucum-orange-violet ramp, orbital systems, and motion grammar, read `visual-patterns.md`. That layer extends composition choices but does not replace these canonical colors.

## Typography

- Use Figtree. Observed weights: ExtraBold, SemiBold, Medium, Regular, and Light.
- Use ExtraBold or SemiBold for display titles; SemiBold or Medium for headings; Regular or Medium for body; Light only for secondary labels.
- Use Arial when Figtree is unavailable. Do not download or synthesize a font without authorization.
- The slide template uses an observed scale including 8, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 42, 48, 56, and 104 pt. Preserve inherited sizes in template-based slides.
- Never typeset a substitute logo.

## Composition

- Build a strong left-aligned hierarchy with generous Gelo/White space.
- Use a two-by-two grid: two major horizontal blocks, two major vertical blocks, then subdivide. Respect margins, anchors, and breathing room.
- Allow some elements to float, but align them to a stronger nearby anchor and keep them inside the grid limits.
- Use restrained rules and flat fields. Use Urucum selectively, not as an all-over fill by default.
- Keep client-facing work concise and polished; operational work dense but scannable; internal guidance warm and direct.
- Prefer one coherent composition over a dashboard of cards, pills, or decorative UI chrome.

## Logo Rules

- Use positive core, shared-icon, and Instituto art in Chumbo on Gelo/White or other light grounds.
- Use negative Gelo art on Chumbo, black, dark imagery, or dark gradient regions.
- Use the Unlimited positive Urucum lockup on light grounds and its negative Gelo lockup on dark grounds.
- Preserve transparency, proportions, orientation, spacing, and the complete lockup.
- Do not recolor, outline, shadow, bevel, stretch, condense, rotate, crop, separate, rearrange, trace, or redraw a mark.
- No numeric clear-space or minimum-size rule appears in the sources. Give the mark visible breathing room and test it at final size.
- Prefer SVG where supplied; otherwise use the high-resolution transparent PNG.

## Copy and Claims

- Spell the organization name `Kunumi`.
- Preserve the user’s requested language. Source guides demonstrate conversational Brazilian Portuguese for internal guidance.
- Prefer short headings, clear verbs, and approachable explanations.
- Do not invent a legal name, tagline, slogan, certification, customer claim, performance claim, product promise, or relationship between the three identities.
- Do not copy Slack policy, support channels, employee instructions, or dated product references into unrelated artifacts.

## Final Gate

Verify identity choice, source asset, contrast variant, proportions, typography, grid, spacing, text fit, image resolution, motion-loop legibility, links, alt text, and placeholder removal. Check every final frame/page/slide at delivery size.
